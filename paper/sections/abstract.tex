Learned samplers are increasingly proposed for equilibrium sampling and free energies in chemistry, and they are usually judged by self-normalized diagnostics such as the effective sample size (ESS). We show with an elementary proposition that these diagnostics are structurally invariant to the Boltzmann mass $\alpha$ of unvisited basins. Conditioned on not proposing a basin, the importance weights follow the law of the truncated system: the ESS stays high while the free energy shifts by $-k_BT\log(1-\alpha)$ and the nominal delta-method interval contracts around the biased value, so coverage falls toward zero while the basin stays unvisited. In a toy double well where one basin holds $\vtoyMassTwo\%$ of the mass, a proposal putting mass $\vbdEps$ on that basin achieves a median ESS of $\vbdESS\%$ but covers the exact $\log Z$ in $\vbdCov\%$ of $\vbdRuns$ runs. On exactly enumerated Sherrington-Kirkpatrick instances ($n=24$), nominal 95\% intervals from masked diffusion and a GFlowNet cover the truth in only $\vcovDiffTwo\%$ and $\vcovGFNTwo\%$ of runs at $\beta=2$, while classical estimators and an autoregressive network (VAN-MADE) stay near 95\%. A reverse check that scores the proposal on draws from an independent reference sampler flags the dropped basin where the forward Jensen gap is blind. A RealNVP flow trained by reverse KL on an analytic surrogate of the alanine dipeptide Ramachandran surface drops the $C_{7\rm ax}$ basin ($\valaAlpha\%$ of the surrogate's mass) and reports a median ESS of $\valaESS\%$ with $\valaCov\%$ coverage; the reverse check gives $G_{\rm rev}=\valaGrev$\,nats. Flows on model landscapes in up to 414 dimensions, including the real LJ$_{38}$ potential, behave the same way, although one control with the correct support also miscovers, so a large reverse gap is not specific to a missing basin. Finally, all-atom simulations of chignolin and Trp-cage and a Langevin sweep of LJ$_{38}$ size the effect and show its single-trajectory analogue: an unbiased run reports nothing about basins it has not reached. Omitting the Trp-cage native basin, $\vtrpNative\%$ of our trajectory, would shift the free energy by $\vtrpBias$\,kcal/mol.
