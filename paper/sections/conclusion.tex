Self-normalized diagnostics such as the effective sample size and delta-method confidence intervals certify that a proposal fits the regions it visits, and nothing else (Proposition~\ref{prop:unvisited}). When a generative sampler omits an entropic or energetic basin carrying Boltzmann mass $\alpha$, the ESS remains high and nominal intervals shrink around a biased estimate, collapsing empirical coverage toward zero.

Against exact combinatorial ground truth on spin glasses, masked diffusion and GFlowNets exhibit severe calibration collapse in the frustrated phase (covering the true free energy in only $\vcovDiffTwo\%$ and $\vcovGFNTwo\%$ of runs at $\beta=2$), while VAN-MADE and the classical interval estimators (AIS, SMC, PA) stay near 95\%. A flow on an analytic alanine surrogate drops the $C_{7\rm ax}$ basin ($\valaAlpha\%$ of the surrogate's mass) while reporting an ESS of $\valaESS\%$ and coverage of $\valaCov\%$, and flows on model landscapes in up to 414 dimensions behave the same way. The reverse check flags these omissions, but a control with the correct support can also give a large reverse gap.

In molecular units the shift depends on the dropped basin's mass: omitting the Trp-cage native basin, $\vtrpNative\%$ of our trajectory, would shift $F$ by $\vtrpBias$\,kcal/mol, while a basin of a few percent shifts it by a few hundredths. Unbiased MD trajectories share the blind spot: they report nothing about funnels they fail to reach.

Our findings suggest that self-reported forward metrics should be supplemented with independent bidirectional checks, particularly when evaluating samplers on complex physical systems where ground truth is unknown, and that new architectures should be tested against solvable references where mode drop can be proven.

\paragraph{Recommended evaluation protocol for practitioners.}
To prevent false confidence in scientific applications, we recommend a four-point evaluation protocol:
(1)~\emph{Do not rely on forward metrics alone}: high ESS certifies only local fit within visited basins, providing zero signal of omitted Boltzmann mass.
(2)~\emph{Deploy bidirectional checks}: evaluate the reverse gap $G_{\rm rev}$ against an offline library of independent reference configurations (with mixing verified via $\hat{R} < 1.05$) before high-throughput deployment.
(3)~\emph{Pre-flight audit on solvable exact references}: benchmark new neural architectures on frustrated systems with exact partition functions (such as SpinGlassBench or low-dimensional surrogates solved by quadrature) to expose mode truncation.
(4)~\emph{Check populations against converged data}: compare basin fractions with enhanced-sampling or long equilibrium simulations of the same system and force field, not with single trajectories.
Crucially, self-normalized diagnostics should never be treated as proof of convergence without orthogonal validation.
